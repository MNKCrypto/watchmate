How it works

1 · Tell it onceType or send a voice note on Telegram; Muse Voice Transcribe + Muse Spark turn it into a profile.
2 · It watches everythingMuse Spark 1.2 watches every 6-second segment, picture and sound together.
3 · It understands the whole broadcastOne long-context Muse Spark 1.3 call turns hundreds of notes into moments.
4 · It decides who it's forEach subscriber is matched or skipped, with a reason.
5 · It checks its own workEvery chosen clip is cut and re-watched: pass, retitle, or replace.
6 · It delivers and keeps talkingCover (Muse Image), reel and recap on Telegram; follow-ups by text or voice.
